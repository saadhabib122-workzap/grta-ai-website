01

Context

The current site is a single dark landing page that says “assemble expert teams in hours.” GRTA has grown into far more than that, and the site needs to show it.

What GRTA is today

Philosophy

AI + BI + CI = DI. Artificial, Business and Collective Intelligence add up to Decision Intelligence. “Be GRTA Together.” People come before technology.

Product

A decision acceleration engine for team builders: understand real competencies, find complementary people, assemble the right team around a problem, and learn from every outcome.

Method: HIIT Engine 9 → 3 → 1

Problem → 9 complementary competencies → 3 parallel HIIT meetings with 20×20 presentations → 1 execution plan. From emergency to execution plan in 3 hours.

Adoption

Organisation Cookbook (10 steps), Profile Recipe (the signals AI needs about a person) and the 7×7 Network (one guide helps six, and that guide sits in another group of seven).

The product roadmap the site must tell

SprintCapabilityWhat it means for a team builder
1Zero blank page + availabilityWrite a messy paragraph, get a structured brief. Busy people are excluded or clearly flagged.
2Gap detection + coverage mapThe AI spots missing expertise before matching. A colour-coded map shows team strength at a glance.
3Plain-language skill translation“I turn complexity into clarity” becomes matchable competencies, backed by past work. The linchpin.
4Explainable matching, team variants, wildcardsExecution, Challenger or Innovation teams, plus an unexpected candidate with evidence for why.
5Data flywheelA two-minute outcome pulse after each project improves every future match.
6Proactive scoutSkills surplus, skills gap alerts against strategy, and cross-silo opportunity spotting.

The moat is the data, not the algorithm. Competitors can copy an interface. They cannot copy years of an organisation's outcome history. The site should make that clear.

Who the site is for

HR & People teams

Want to find, activate and keep hidden competencies inside the organisation.

Project & team builders

Need the right people around a specific problem, fast, without politics or guesswork.

Universities & partners

DTU Skylab as a development partner, universities in Uganda, DTU Science Park.

02

Principles & working rules

People before technology

AI is part of the solution but never dominates the visual story. People and relationships lead.

Clear entry points

HR and project teams each find their own challenges and use cases within seconds. Partners get their own track.

Nordic simplicity

Generous white space, short messages, a limited palette, and a calm, trustworthy experience.

How we work, so little needs redoing

  • Approval gates. Brand, then sitemap and copy, are approved before any page is built.
  • Design system first. Pages are composed only from approved components, so a change happens in one place.
  • Single sources of truth. Colours and type live in brand/tokens.json; all copy lives in messages/en.json.
  • Drop-in illustrations. Every illustration slot has a fixed ratio and filename, so final art replaces placeholders without layout changes.
  • Self-review after every edit. Typecheck, lint, screenshots on desktop and mobile, copy check. Fix before moving on.

Phase 1: Brand assets

Approval gate

“Nordic Futurism.” Warm people, luminous threads. The old navy and orange style is retired; only the GR/TA block mark carries over.

Concept. People and relationships stay at the centre: warm, tactile, calm. Futurism appears only as connection: thin luminous threads, constellation nodes and soft holographic overlays linking people. These are how we draw Collective Intelligence. AI never takes the foreground.

Attributes: Human · Connected · Calm · Clear · Quietly ahead.

Logo system: the GR/TA block mark, redrawn

  • A vector redraw of the 2×2 stacked “GR / TA” block: geometric slab letters on an even grid, with corners tuned for small sizes.
  • Variants: primary (Ember letters on Ink), reversed (Ink on Birch), mono Forest, mono white, and a horizontal lockup (mark + “grta.ai”).
  • Living mark: a motion version where a luminous thread traces between the four letters, used for the loader and hero intro. Static everywhere else.
  • Deliverables: SVG masters, PNG exports from 16 to 1024 px, favicon, Apple touch icon, default social image, clear-space and minimum-size rules.

A dark mode, “Nordic night” (Ink background, Fjord threads), is defined in the tokens from day one. Every text and background pair is checked against WCAG AA by script.

Typography (free Google Fonts)

Geist Mono, data and labels. Scores, coverage values, small uppercase labels. The quiet “machine” voice, used sparingly.

Illustration system: “Polished clay”

  • Style: soft 3D characters with matte materials in Scandinavian interiors (birch wood, linen, plants, daylight). Calm, mature expressions, never cartoon grins.
  • Futurist layer: fine fjord-blue light threads connecting people; small holographic coverage maps floating above tables.
  • Recurring cast: six named characters across ages, backgrounds and roles: HR lead, project lead, data scientist, operations analyst (“the wildcard”), student, and a 7×7 guide. They appear throughout, so the site reads as one story.
  • Illustration bible (brand/illustration-bible.md): style rules, master prompt, about 14 scene prompts mapped to the sitemap, lighting and palette locks, do and don't examples.

Limitation. Raster illustrations can't be generated in this environment. They are produced from the bible (Midjourney, Adobe Firefly or a designer). Until then, slots show on-brand SVG placeholders: silhouettes joined by threads. Swapping in final art changes no layout.

Other brand assets

  • Icon set: about 30 line icons (1.5 px stroke, rounded) for the 9 competencies, the roadmap capabilities and the UI. SVG sprite and React components.
  • Motifs: thread and constellation pattern; coverage-map language (strong, adequate, gap as Moss, Fjord, Ember dots); the 20×20 slide-stack.
  • Voice and tone guide: plain language, short sentences, no em dashes. Claim: “People move work forward.”
  • Brand guidelines page and PDF, plus brand/tokens.json as the single source for the site.

Phase 4: Signature features

The site doesn't just describe GRTA; it lets visitors use slices of it. Each feature shows one capability of the real product.

Nordlys Group. All demos run against a fictional organisation of about 80 synthetic people, with profiles, past projects, availability and outcome history. No real personal data is needed, which keeps every demo GDPR-safe.

1

Zero Blank Page /lab#brief · also on Home

Type a messy paragraph about a problem. Watch it turn into a structured brief (roles, competencies, timeline) in real time. Then GRTA taps you on the shoulder: “Projects like this usually need change management. You asked for none.”

Why newThe AI critiques your thinking, not just your formatting.

2

Buzzword Translator /lab#translate

Paste your own corporate self-description (“I turn complexity into clarity”). Get matchable competencies, each with the reasoning, plus a shareable “Translated” card for LinkedIn.

Why newTurns the product's linchpin into something people want to share.

3

Team Variants & the Wildcard /lab#team

Pick a brief, then flip between Execution, Challenger and Innovation teams from Nordlys. A live coverage map shows strengths and gaps. A Wildcard card argues “I know this looks odd, but hear me out” with evidence. Unavailable people appear greyed out, with the reason.

Why newExplainable matching you can feel: choices, not a black box.

4

9 → 3 → 1 Simulator /lab#hiit

Enter a problem. GRTA picks 9 competency lenses, splits them into 3 parallel rooms (an animated constellation), each room returns a short perspective, and they converge into 1 draft execution plan with owners, deadlines and KPIs. Downloadable as a PDF.

Why newThe HIIT Engine, compressed into sixty seconds.

5

7×7 Network Grower /lab#network

Enter your organisation's size. A constellation grows from one guide to the whole organisation, showing how many guides and layers you need and how long rollout takes.

Why newMakes the human adoption model tangible for HR buyers.

6

Living Constellation Home hero background

Each anonymised, moderated problem from the Lab adds a glowing node to a shared constellation, linked by the competencies it needed. Visitors see what others are working on.

Why newCollective Intelligence as a live, shared artwork.

7

Challenge Exchange /partners · section

Universities and partners (DTU, Uganda) post a challenge. GRTA tags the competencies and disciplines needed and surfaces complementary challenges and partners across borders. Partners sign in by magic link.

Why newA first live slice of the cross-silo scout, built for the DTU conversation.

8

Two-Minute Pulse end of every demo

Five questions about the demo itself, with a visible timer that stays under two minutes.

Why newDemonstrates the data flywheel while collecting real feedback.

Launch order: 1, 3, 2, 4 at launch; 5, 6, 7, 8 shortly after. Every feature has a static fallback when the AI is unavailable or rate-limited.

Phase 5: Backend

Supabase (Postgres, auth, storage, pgvector) with Next.js route handlers and the Claude API.

Data model

TableHolds
leadsDemo and partner requests (HR, project, partner, investor), consent flag, source page.
synthetic_people · synthetic_projects · availability · outcomesThe Nordlys Group dataset, seeded from a JSON file.
competenciesTaxonomy based on the 9 HIIT competencies and sub-competencies, with vector embeddings.
lab_sessionsAnonymised demo inputs and outputs (opt-in), for the constellation and analytics.
constellation_nodesModerated public nodes: pending, approved or hidden.
challenges · partner_orgs · challenge_matchesThe Challenge Exchange.
pulses · rate_limitsTwo-minute ratings; per-visitor usage counters.

Row-level security on every table: the public can only insert where intended; private tables are readable by admins only.

AI layer

  • Claude Sonnet 5.5 for briefs, translation, team rationale and HIIT synthesis; Claude Haiku 5.5 for tagging, moderation and cheap classification.
  • Every AI output follows a strict schema, so the interface never guesses at free text. Responses stream so demos feel alive.
  • Matching is deterministic: vector similarity on competencies, an availability filter, and a diversity term for Challenger and Innovation teams. The AI only explains the result. That keeps it consistent and cheap.
  • Cost and abuse protection: Cloudflare Turnstile, per-visitor rate limits, input length caps, a daily spend cap, and prompt caching.

Supporting services

  • Email: Resend (free tier) for lead notifications; partner magic links via Supabase Auth.
  • Lead continuity: new leads also sync to the existing Google Sheet, so the current workflow keeps working.
  • Analytics: cookie-free (Vercel Analytics or Umami), so no cookie banner.
  • Admin (/admin): magic-link login for allow-listed emails; leads, moderation queues, demo usage and AI spend.

Review protocol, costs, inputs

Self-review after every edit

  1. Typecheck and lint must pass.
  2. Screenshots of every changed route at 1440 px and 390 px, light and dark, checked for contrast, spacing, overflow, clipping and leftover placeholders.
  3. Copy check: plain language, no em dashes, matches the voice guide.
  4. Fix, re-screenshot, and only then move on.
