# GRTA Voice & Tone

## Claim

**People move work forward.**

Supporting lines (use one at a time, never stacked):
- The right people. The right perspectives. At the right time.
- From problem to plan, together.
- Be GRTA together.
- AI connects data. GRTA connects people.

## How we sound

| We are | So we write | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Human** | "Find the people who can help." | "Leverage human capital assets." |
| **Clear** | One idea per sentence. Short words. | Stacked clauses and jargon. |
| **Calm** | Confident, without hype. | "Revolutionary," "game-changing," exclamation marks. |
| **Honest about AI** | "GRTA suggests. You decide." | "Our AI picks your perfect team." |
| **Warm** | "Ingrid, here's someone you may not know yet." | "User 4471: match found." |

## Rules

1. **No em dashes.** Use a full stop, colon or comma instead.
2. **Plain language first.** If a term is needed (Collective Intelligence, 20×20), explain it in the same sentence the first time it appears on a page.
3. **People are the subject.** "Your team finds…", not "The algorithm finds…".
4. **Show, don't claim.** Prefer a concrete example ("a logistics analyst who stress-tests launches") over an adjective ("innovative matching").
5. **Numbers in numerals.** 3 hours, 9 competencies, 20×20, 7×7.
6. **British English spelling** on the English site (organisation, colour), matching the founders' material.
7. **Sentence case** for headings and buttons. Buttons start with a verb: "Book a demo," "Try the Lab," "See how it works."
8. **Danish later:** keep sentences short and avoid idioms, so translation stays faithful.

## Names and terms

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| GRTA (in text), grta.ai (in the lockup and URLs) | Grta, G.R.T.A. |
| Collective Intelligence (CI) | crowd wisdom, hive mind |
| team builder | resource manager, requester |
| competency / competencies | skill sets, capabilities (for people) |
| HIIT Engine, 9 → 3 → 1 | sprint meeting, workshop |
| 7×7 Network, GRTA guide | ambassador programme, super-user |
| Lab | demo centre, sandbox |

## Microcopy examples

- Empty state: "Nothing here yet. Describe a problem and we'll find the people."
- Error: "That didn't work on our side. Try again in a moment."
- Rate limit: "You've tried a lot today. Book a demo to see the full version."
- Gap detection: "Projects like this usually need change management. You haven't asked for any. Add it?"
- Wildcard: "This one may look unusual. Here's why we think Lars could help."
